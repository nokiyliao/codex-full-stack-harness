# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically honest community.
Contributors, maintainers, and reviewers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully;
- disclose limitations, uncertainty, and attribution clearly;
- protect private reports, credentials, task data, and personal information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and
  deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces,
and public interactions when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the contact method on
their repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a
public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, moderate discussions, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a
participant. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented
privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where another maintainer is
available.

Good-faith disagreement about technical claims is not misconduct. Harassment,
retaliation, or knowingly exposing private material is.
